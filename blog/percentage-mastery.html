Percentage Mastery

Percentages appear in shopping discounts, interest calculations, tips, and tax estimates. A solid understanding of them can make everyday money decisions much easier.

The most important formula is $\text{percentage} = \frac{\text{part}}{\text{whole}} \times 100$. This lets you convert a part of a whole into a percent, which is useful whether you are evaluating a sale or comparing two values.

When you want to find a percent of a number, multiply the number by the percent as a decimal. For example, $15\%$ of $200$ is $0.15 \times 200 = 30$. With a calculator, this job is quick, but understanding the logic behind it makes the result much easier to trust.
