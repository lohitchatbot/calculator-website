Math Tricks for Speed

One of the best ways to improve mental math is to learn a few reliable shortcuts. Rounding numbers, splitting them into easier parts, and using familiar benchmark values can turn a hard-looking problem into a quick estimate.

For example, multiplying by $25$ can be done by dividing by $4$ and then multiplying by $100$. This trick saves time in school or when working through daily calculations. With practice, these patterns become automatic and make arithmetic feel less intimidating.

These methods also help you check a calculator result. If the number seems wildly different from your estimate, it is worth rechecking the input before moving on.
